## Gaps the review found that are real

1. **Version had two homes and both lied.** The session stamp came
   from a hardcoded `VERSION = "2.5.3"` in the source that releases
   stopped bumping, while `coach_config.json` carried a second,
   unread `version` key. Fixed in v2.8.0: the code constant is the
   single source; the config key is gone. (This is also why recent
   session logs stamped "2.5.3" on current code.)
2. **Replay sampling too thin.** Frames were recorded at 10 Hz; the
   shortest analyzed events (0.18 s lockups) are 2 samples at that
   rate. v2.8.0 records at the full acquisition rate (~30 Hz).
3. **No cue memory.** A persistent leak earned the same approach cue
   every lap. v2.8.0: an identical approach cue at a corner is
   suppressed for 3 laps unless the loss worsened by 0.15 s+.
4. **No workload gate on speech timing.** Approach cues fired at a
   fixed distance before the brake zone, even mid-corner in linked
   sequences. v2.8.0: deferrable speech (approach cues, guide tips)
   waits for a straight — it holds while the car is inside any corner
   zone and drops silently inside 100 m of the brake point. Exit
   feedback and summaries are positional by construction and exempt.
5. **Braking analysis was point-only.** Brake point delta + lockup,
   but not how the pedal was used. v2.8.0 adds peak-brake and
   brake-release-duration comparisons, spoken only with a
   corroborating symptom (the review's own evidence rule).

## Deliberate non-goals (reaffirmed)

- **Composite reference as the live target.** The tool's founding
  decision (the driver's own, Nov 2026) is the proven-achievable
  self-best lap; the composite exists as report information
  (theoretical best), never as the spoken benchmark.
- **Race strategy / traffic coaching.** Out of scope for a
  practice/hotlap tool; traffic-lap *exclusion* (data hygiene, not
  strategy) stays a candidate for when the driver laps in traffic.
- **Racing-line coaching.** No validated track-edge data source yet;
  the review itself says not to coach geometry until positional data
  is validated. The dead-reckoned outline is display-only.
- **LLM at runtime.** Agreed with the review: deterministic core
  first; language belongs to post-session artifacts, if anywhere.
- **Module split / PyInstaller.** Single file is the deployment
  model (one pull, double-click); an unsigned exe trips Smart App
  Control harder than the script does.
